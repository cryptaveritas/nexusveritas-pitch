The Problem

Most token scanners focus on isolated technical checks. Modern scams combine multiple risk factors that individually may appear harmless — but together form a measurable risk profile.

Traditional scanners answer:

"Is this token technically safe?"

NexusVeritas answers:

"Who created it? Who controls it? Can liquidity disappear? Does creator behavior indicate elevated risk?"
