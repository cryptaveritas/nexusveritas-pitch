Methodology Notes

Attribution decay: Insider funding relationships are time-sensitive. Once coordinated wallets exit positions, they leave the top-10 holder list and the funding relationship becomes undetectable through current holder analysis. Historical snapshots are therefore not optional — they are evidence preservation.

Infrastructure wallets: Large exchange withdrawal wallets (1M+ SOL, mass distribution) can create false attribution signals by appearing as a shared funding source. Always verify creator-level attribution before confirming operator links.

Creator attribution: Creator wallet addresses proved more durable than holder-based funding attribution. While insider relationships disappeared after exits, creator relationships remained observable and allowed historical linkage to be preserved.
